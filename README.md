# Pigeon Post

Write it down. Post it to yourself.

Pigeon Post is a small journaling app for people who keep their diary in their inbox. You write freely, pick a look for the entry, add a photo or a stamp if you like, and send the finished letter to your own email. Your inbox becomes a searchable, good-looking archive of your days.

There are no accounts and no sign-up. Your entries are never uploaded anywhere. Everything happens in your browser.

## Why it exists

Emailing yourself diary entries works, but a plain email is a plain email. Pigeon Post keeps the part you like (a dated, searchable record in your own inbox) and makes the writing and the result feel like a journal instead of a message box.

## Writing an entry

- Give the entry a title if you want one.
- The date defaults to today and can be changed, so you can log a day you missed.
- Add a location. It shows up in the header of your letter.
- Add tags by typing a word and pressing Enter. Tags appear as small pills in the letter header, which makes entries easy to find later.
- Write in a plain, distraction-free page. The word count sits underneath.

Everything else (templates, photos, stamp, sign-off icon) lives in collapsible sections below the page, so the writing space stays quiet until you want more.

## Templates

Each template gives the email a different look. The header always reads the same way, `16 Oct, 2026. | 📍 Location`, so your entries stay consistent and searchable whatever style you pick.

| Template | The look |
| --- | --- |
| Typewriter | A typed memo on cream paper, ending with a classic "— 30 —" |
| Fall | Rust, gold and woodsmoke |
| Spring | Soft greens and blossom pink |
| Field Notes | Kraft cover with a boxed header strip |
| Old Letter | Parchment, "Dear me," and a sign-off |
| Postcard | A banner, your message on the left and an address side on the right |
| Food Diary | A menu card with a star rating and a place sticker, like the location tag on an Instagram story |
| Letterboxd | A dark film log with a title, year and half-star ratings. You can mark a rewatch, add who you watched with, and tick "Watched in theaters" |
| Bookstore Haul | A receipt for your new books, one per line, with an item count |
| Winter | Cool blues and quiet type |
| Plain | Clean and simple |

Food Diary, Letterboxd and Bookstore Haul add a few extra fields for the details that suit them.

## Photos

Add up to eight photos. They are resized automatically so the email stays a sensible size, and each template frames them in its own style.

## Postage stamp maker

Pick any photo and crop it into a perforated postage stamp. Drag to reposition, zoom, and choose portrait or landscape. The stamp goes in the top right of your letter, and the app remembers it for next time.

## Sign-off icon

Upload your own small icons, such as a set of little flowers, and choose one as your signature. Each template places it differently: centred under the Typewriter's "— 30 —", next to "— me" on the Old Letter, in the corner of the Postcard, and so on. Transparent PNGs look best. Your icons stay on your device.

## The subject line

The subject fills itself in as `16 Oct, 2026 | Your Title`, or just the date when you haven't given the entry a title. You can edit it freely before sending, and a small "Auto" link puts the automatic version back.

## Sending it to yourself

The letter is styled with formatting that a "send email" link can't carry, so the app uses a short copy-and-paste step instead.

1. Type your address in the To field at the top of the letter.
2. Press the wax seal. It copies your finished letter, photos and all.
3. Open a blank email with the Gmail or Mail app tab. The To line and subject are already filled in.
4. Paste into the body and send.

Nothing is sent until you press send in your own mail app.

Under "Other ways to keep it" you can also download the entry as an `.eml` file (a complete email with photos, stamp and icon embedded), copy just the subject, or copy a plain-text version.

## Your privacy

- No account, no tracking, no server storing your writing.
- Drafts, your stamp and your sign-off icons are saved in your browser on your own device, so they are there when you come back. Photos are not saved between visits.
- Tap "New entry" when you want a clean page. It asks you to confirm before clearing.
- Clearing your browser data clears your saved drafts and icons.

## Good to know

- It works in any modern browser, on desktop or phone, and follows your light or dark mode.
- Copying with formatting depends on your browser allowing it. If it can't, the app tells you and points you to the `.eml` download instead.
- How a pasted letter looks depends on your mail app. Simple colours, fonts and tables are used so it holds up in most of them, but styling can vary from app to app.

## Under the hood

A single HTML file with no build step and no dependencies beyond Google Fonts. Everything else is inline.
